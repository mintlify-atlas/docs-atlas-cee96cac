AirDrawio is a browser-based diagram editor you control with a webcam and your voice. Point your index finger to move the cursor, say **"select"** or **"hold"** to click and drag, and draw shapes in the air with the Air Pen — all without touching a mouse or keyboard. The editor runs on [maxGraph](https://maxgraph.github.io/maxGraph/), the maintained TypeScript successor to the mxGraph engine, so every shape, handle, connector, and undo action is fully real.

## How it works

AirDrawio splits spatial input from discrete actions. Your index fingertip moves the cursor continuously at camera frame rate. Voice commands supply the button presses — **"hold"** to start a drag, **"release"** to drop, **"rectangle"** to create a shape at the pointer. This split keeps targeting fast while avoiding accidental triggers during precise movement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and start">
    Clone the repo, run `npm install`, then `npm run dev`. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173` in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Gesture Mode">
    Click **Gesture** in the top bar and allow camera and microphone access when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the model">
    The status bar shows **Hand + local voice ready** when MediaPipe and the Vosk command model have finished loading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start diagramming">
    Point your index finger to move the on-screen cursor, then say **"rectangle"** to drop a shape, **"hold"** to drag it, and **"release"** to drop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Voice commands are decoded entirely in the browser using a bundled Vosk WebAssembly model. No audio is sent to any external service.
</Note>
